## Purpose

Replace the two per-path Triton top-k implementations in the MiniMax-M3 MSA (SM100/Blackwell) indexer with a **single MSA `sparse_topk_select`** over one unified score buffer shared by decode and prefill.

**Before**, the MSA indexer ran two independent top-k paths:
- **decode** → T
