## Summary

Adds `kpool_topk_transform` as a lightweight sglang JIT kernel. It selects the top-k pooled groups from a `[B, num_groups]` score tensor at pool granularity, expands each selected group to `pool_size` token indices, and optionally remaps those indices through a page table or a ragged off
